About

I am a first-year Ph.D. student in Information, Risk, and Operations Management (IROM) at the McCombs School of Business, The University of Texas at Austin, advised by Prof. Mingyuan Zhou.

I received my B.S. in Information Engineering from Shanghai Jiao Tong University in 2026, where I worked with Prof. Jiangchao Yao. During my undergraduate studies, I was also fortunate to work remotely with Prof. Mingyuan Zhou and Huangjie Zheng at UT Austin.

My research interests lie in generative models, especially diffusion models, and representation learning. I want to explore:

  • How generative models, especially diffusion and autoregressive models, can achieve better and faster performance in various downstream tasks;
  • How synthetic data produced by generative models, or the representations they learn, can assist representation learning.

Publications

* denotes equal contribution

BLaDe
Bayesian Language Decision: Looking Back and Planning Ahead
Haicheng Huang*, Lang Lin*, Xinyang Liu*, Yanbo Wang, Tianyu Chen, Ruyi An, Mingyuan Zhou
Preprint2026

A training-free framework that pairs a fast System I actor with a System II planner by treating each decision as Bayesian inference over actions — recent history forms the prior and the planned future serves as evidence. Across three actors and four interactive benchmarks, BLaDe raises average success from 14.76% to 40.38% without updating the actor.

Wide-In, Narrow-Out: Revokable Decoding for Efficient and Effective DLLMs
Feng Hong*, Geng Yu*, Yushi Ye, Haicheng Huang, Huangjie Zheng, Ya Zhang, Yanfeng Wang, Jiangchao Yao
ICLR 2026 NeurIPS 2025 Workshop · Spotlight
arXiv

A training-free decoding algorithm that enables revokable decoding in DLLMs: it aggressively drafts multiple tokens in parallel while using the model's bidirectional context to verify and re-mask suspicious ones for refinement.

Generative Data Augmentation via Diffusion Distillation, Adversarial Alignment, and Importance Reweighting
Ruyi An*, Haicheng Huang*, Huangjie Zheng, Mingyuan Zhou
NeurIPS 2025
Paper

DAR-GDA unites model Distillation, Adversarial alignment, and importance Reweighting to make diffusion-quality augmentation both fast and optimized for downstream learning, matching or exceeding large web-pretrained text-to-image models while using only in-domain data.
